## About me

I'm an **Information Systems undergraduate at the Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU)**, focused on software development and the engineering behind reliable applications.

My main area of interest is **Java Back-End**, especially APIs, data modeling and software architecture. I also work with front-end technologies to connect the different parts of an application.

Here I share university projects, technical experiments and applications built around practical challenges.

> **My approach:** clear code, sound fundamentals and decisions that make software easier to maintain.
