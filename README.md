### A little about me

I'm Artur, an Information Systems student at the **Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU)**.

I'm interested in the part of software development that happens beyond the screen: how data is organized, how systems communicate, and why some solutions are easier to maintain than others.

**Java back-end is my main direction.** I'm building my foundation in programming, object-oriented design, algorithms, and databases. Exploring front-end development helps me see how those pieces come together in a real application.

This GitHub is a record of that process — including experiments, class projects, and ideas that are still taking shape.

### The way I want to build

I'm trying to get better at asking more than *“Does it run?”*

- **Can I explain it?** Understanding the solution matters more than copying one.
- **Can someone else read it?** Clear names and simple structure go a long way.
- **Can it evolve?** Small decisions about data and responsibilities matter later.

I still have plenty to learn. That's exactly why I'm here.
